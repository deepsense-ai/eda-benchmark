2. **Invalid Date Corrections**:
   - The timesheet contained non-existent dates `2025-04-31` and `2025-06-31`. These were corrected to the end of their respective calendar months: `2025-04-30` and `2025-06-30`.

3. **Billing Periods Boundary**:
   - The earliest timesheet record starts on `2025-03-04`, which is a **Tuesday**.
   - As per instructions, each monthly billing period starts on the same day of the week (the first Tuesday of each calendar month) when employees physically collect their salaries:
     - **March Period**: `2025-03-04` to `2025-03-31`
     - **April Period**: `2025-04-01` to `2025-05-05` (5-week cycle)
     - **May Period**: `2025-05-06` to `2025-06-02` (4-week cycle)
     - **June Period**: `2025-06-03` to `2025-06-30` (4-week cycle)

4. **Exclusions of Inconsistent / Invalid Records**:
   - Records with negative bonuses (exactly 7 records) had 0 hours worked and empty task lists, and were excluded as highly corrupt and uncorrectable.
   - Record `id=342` had 0 hours worked but positive completed tasks, which is structurally inconsistent (cannot perform tasks in 0 hours). It was excluded from the final payroll.

5. **Bonus & Total Salary Recalculation**:
   - Mismatches in the raw `bonus` column in timesheets (such as arbitrary additions/reductions or positive bonuses when tasks were empty) were corrected by recalculating bonuses directly from the completed `performed_tasks` list in timesheets and the official rates in `tasks.csv` (excluding any invalid/undefined task IDs).
