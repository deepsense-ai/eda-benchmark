   * **Exact Row Deduplication:** I first deduplicated `timesheet.csv` to remove exact row copies.
   * **Employee ID Correction:** The 5 invalid IDs were mapped to their correct PESEL counterparts.
   * **Merging Shifts:** Since the overlapping valid/invalid records have different performed tasks but identical hours and facility, they represent split tasks for the same day. I merged them by taking the `max(hours_worked)` (since they represent the same hours worked once) but **summing** the task bonuses from both rows.
